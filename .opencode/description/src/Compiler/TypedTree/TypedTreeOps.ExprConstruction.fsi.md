# TypedTreeOps.ExprConstruction.fsi

**Purpose**: Contract for the typed-tree construction library: ordering comparers, lambda/let/loop/record/union/tuple construction helpers, CCU/`ValReprInfo` accessors, and the large auto-opened family of `is*Ty`/`dest*Ty`/`mk*Ty` type predicates and constructors (the `TypeTesters` module), plus the small `TypedTreeCollections` (`ValHash`/`ValMultiMap`/`TyconRefMultiMap`) and `CommonContainers` support modules.
